Sound by Sound has moved

Blendaroo is now Sound by Sound, at soundbysound.app. Taking you there, with your reader’s words and aliens…

This home-screen icon can’t hand its saved progress to the new address by itself. To bring the words and aliens along:

  1. Copy the link below.
  2. In Safari, go to soundbysound.app, then Share > Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Sound by Sound from the new icon, then Settings (the gear) > Add from a link, and paste.
  4. Once your aliens are there, you can delete this old icon.
